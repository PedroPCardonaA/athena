# Code of Conduct

Athena is a small, friendly research tool. Everyone taking part in this project (issues, pull
requests, discussions) is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Our standard
- Be kind and assume good faith; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it specifically and helpfully.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes,
publishing someone's private information, spam, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks these rules. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer [@PedroPCardonaA](https://github.com/PedroPCardonaA) through GitHub (for sensitive
reports use a private security advisory on this repository, "Security" tab). Reports are handled
confidentially.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.
